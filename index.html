Our Heritage & Mission

Crafting community through ethical coffee rituals.

At Café Xixi, we believe coffee is more than just a morning routine—it is a sacred daily ritual. Founded with a passion for clean aesthetics and ethical sourcing, we partner directly with independent family farms across Latin America and East Africa.

Our mission is simple: serve exceptional, sustainably grown coffee in a warm, minimalist environment that fosters genuine human connection and lingering conversations.

Master Craftsmanship

We meticulously measure, grind, and brew every single cup with calibrated precision to highlight peak natural notes.

Direct Ethical Trade

Direct partnerships ensuring living wages and environmental stewardship for coffee farming families worldwide.

Inclusive Space

Designing welcoming, minimalist sanctuaries crafted for shared moments, quiet contemplation, and creative ideas.
