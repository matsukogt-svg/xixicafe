Our Story & Mission

At Café Xixi, we believe coffee is more than just a morning routine—it is a ritual. Founded with a passion for clean aesthetics and ethical sourcing, we partner directly with independent farmers across Latin America and East Africa.

Our mission is to serve exceptional, sustainably sourced coffee in a warm, minimalist environment that fosters community and genuine connection.
